title: DHL Express customs paperwork checklist
updated: 2026-10-20
source: carrier-partner-feed
---
For DHL Express international shipments, include a commercial invoice with an accurate description, quantity, value and country of origin for each item. Add HS codes to avoid customs holds.

Shipments with incomplete paperwork may be returned to the sender at the sender's cost.
